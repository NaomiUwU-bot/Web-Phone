Our story, in short.

DePhone started as a single counter in a shopping street, selling unlocked phones to people tired of long contracts and confusing fine print. Today we're a small team of phone specialists who test, compare, and stock devices we'd actually want to use ourselves.

Every phone that leaves our shop is checked for battery health, screen quality, and software updates before it reaches you. If something's not right, we fix it — no fifteen-step return process required.

We believe a phone is one of the most personal things you own, so we take the time to match you with the right device instead of just the most expensive one.
